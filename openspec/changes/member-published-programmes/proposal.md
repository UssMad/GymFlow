# Member published programme access

## Why

Members need a secure account view that turns a coach-approved programme into a day-by-day weekly plan they can follow.

## What changes

- Add member Sanctum authentication.
- Expose the member's current published programme with sessions, exercises, prescription values, and coach notes.
- Expose past published programmes as member-only history.

## Out of scope

- Session completion tracking and programme modification by a member.
